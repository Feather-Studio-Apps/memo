Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

memo. is built to work without an account, and without sending your notes anywhere by default. This page explains exactly what that means.

No account, no sign-up

memo. doesn't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email, or any personal identifier just to use the app.

Your notes stay on your device

Everything you write — notes, folders, tags, voice recordings — is stored locally on your iPhone. We never see it, and it's never sent to Feather Studio's servers, because Feather Studio doesn't operate any servers that store your content.

iCloud Sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync, your notes are synced through your own private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit framework. This data goes directly from your device to your own iCloud storage — Feather Studio has no access to it at any point. iCloud Sync is off by default and is a Pro feature; you can turn it on or off at any time in Settings.

Voice recording and transcription

Voice memos are recorded and stored on your device. Live transcription uses Apple's on-device Speech framework, which processes audio directly on your iPhone rather than sending it to a server. iOS may still require the standard system speech-recognition permission to use this, as it does for any app using this framework — that permission governs the operating system, not anything Feather Studio collects.

In-app purchases

Unlocking Pro is handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Feather Studio never receives your payment details — Apple processes the transaction and simply confirms to the app that it succeeded.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

memo. does not show ads, does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

If you contact us

If you email us for support or feedback, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message. We use this only to respond to you.

Children's privacy

memo. is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and adjust the date above. Continuing to use memo. after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to featherstudio@proton.me.
