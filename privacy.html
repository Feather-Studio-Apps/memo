Privacy Policy  ·  Last updated July 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data Memo. collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

What we collect

Memo. does not collect, store, or share any personal data on our servers. We have no backend, no accounts, and no analytics.

Your notes are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. If you enable iCloud sync (Pro feature), your notes are stored in your private iCloud database — accessible only to you and governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy.

Voice and speech

When you use voice recording, your audio is processed on-device using Apple's Speech Recognition framework. Audio may be sent to Apple's servers for transcription depending on your iOS settings. We never receive, store, or access your audio or transcriptions.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Purchase receipts are verified locally on your device.

iCloud

If you enable iCloud sync, your notes are stored in your private CloudKit database. This data is governed by Apple's privacy policy and is not accessible to us. You can disable iCloud sync at any time from Settings within the app.

Third-party services

Memo. does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children

Memo. does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no content directed at children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at featherstudio@proton.me.
