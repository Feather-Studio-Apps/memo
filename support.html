Frequently Asked Questions

What is Memo.?

Memo. is a minimal note-taking app for iPhone. It lets you capture thoughts quickly — by typing or speaking — and keeps them out of your way until you need them.

How do I create a note?

Tap the Write button on the home screen to create a text note. The keyboard will appear automatically and you can start typing right away.

How do I record a voice note?

Tap the Record button on the home screen or inside the editor. Memo. will transcribe your voice and append it to your note. Voice recording is a Pro feature. Transcription uses your iPhone's language setting — only one language at a time is supported, matching the primary language set on your device.

What is Live Activity?

Live Activity pins a note to your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island so you can see it without opening the app. Tap the ◎ icon on any note card or inside the editor to activate it. Only one note can be live at a time. The Live Activity appearance adapts to the colour of the note card — black or white — to match the look of your note list.

How do I pin a note?

Tap the pin icon on a note card. Pinned notes always appear at the top of your list, sorted by most recently updated.

How do I archive or delete a note?

Swipe left on a note card to reveal Archive and Delete options. You can also open the note and tap the archive icon in the top right. Archived notes can be found in Settings → Archive.

Does Memo. have home screen widgets?

Yes. Memo. offers three home screen widgets — a Write widget, a Voice Note widget, and a combined widget with both. Add them by long pressing your home screen and tapping the + button. The Write widget opens a new note with the keyboard ready. The Voice Note widget opens a new note and starts recording immediately (Pro required). Widgets adapt automatically to your iPhone's light or dark appearance.

Does Memo. support Lock Screen controls?

Yes, on iOS 18 and later. Memo. offers two Lock Screen controls — New Note and Voice Note — available in the bottom corners of your Lock Screen and in Control Centre. Long press your Lock Screen and tap Customise to add them. Tapping a control opens Memo. and goes straight to writing or recording.

What is Memo. Pro?

Memo. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks voice recording and transcription, iCloud sync across your devices, and all future Pro features. There is no subscription — you pay once and it's yours forever.

How does iCloud sync work?

When iCloud sync is enabled (Pro only), your notes are saved to your private iCloud database and synced across all your devices signed into the same Apple ID. Your notes are never shared with anyone.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Memo., tap the Record button or iCloud toggle, and on the paywall screen tap Restore Purchase. Your purchase will be restored automatically.

Can I switch between list and grid view?

Yes. Below the Memo. title on the home screen, tap the list or grid icon to switch between layouts. Your preference is saved between sessions.

How do I contact support?

Tap the Feedback option in Settings to send us an email. We read every message.
