Frequently asked questions

 

What is memo.?

memo. is a simple notes app — write or dictate quick notes, journal entries, or longer-form writing, and keep them organized with folders and tags.

Do I need an account?

No. memo. works fully offline with no sign-up. Turning on iCloud Sync (a Pro feature) uses your own iCloud account — there's no separate memo. account to create.

How do I create a note?

Tap the + button on the Notes tab. You can write directly, or tap the mic to dictate — text and voice both work in the same note.

What's free, and what's Pro?

Writing notes, recording voice memos, folders, tags, formatting (bold, bullets, checklists, photos), and both grid and list views are all free, with no limits. memo. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks live voice transcription (your dictation turned into text as you speak) and iCloud Sync across your devices.

I bought Pro already — how do I get it on a new device?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Since Pro is tied to your Apple ID, it'll unlock automatically on any device signed into the same account.

Where are my notes stored?

On your device, always. If you turn on iCloud Sync, they're also stored in your own private iCloud account, which is how they stay in sync across your devices.

How do folders and tags work?

A note can live in one folder, and carry as many tags as you like. Folders are managed on the Folders tab; tags are created and edited in Settings, and applied to a note from its own tag menu.

I deleted a note by mistake — can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted notes go to Recently Deleted (in Settings) and stay there for 10 days before being permanently removed, giving you time to restore them.

Does memo. show ads or track me?

No. memo. has no ads and no third-party tracking. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
